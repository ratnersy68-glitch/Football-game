## Known limitations
- Signing classes are auto-generated by each staff (placeholder for the recruiting system).
- Top 25 / CFP only include Big Ten, SEC and Big 12 teams; pool opponents can appear in bowls.
- Poll doubles as the CFP selection ranking (no separate committee ranking yet).
- Coaches never change jobs yet (no carousel); your coach can't be fired yet.
- Team passing yards are net of sacks; no redshirts/position changes/promises yet.
- Logos come from ESPN's CDN at runtime; offline or blocked networks show roundels.
